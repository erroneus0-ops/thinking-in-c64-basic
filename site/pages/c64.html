    Special keys
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    On your keyboard: Esc is RUN/STOP and Page Up is RESTORE. RUN/STOP stops a running program. RUN/STOP + RESTORE also tidies the screen and colors, and keeps your program. Show keyboard, under the C64 screen, brings up a C64 keyboard you can tap, for phones and touch screens.
    

    
    The C64 itself
    
    
    
    
    Click the C64 screen before typing, and keep Caps Lock off. Reset clears the program from memory, so submit first.
